THEMES

Tap a theme to read what it covers.

ENTREPRENEURSHIP

Turning ideas into ventures that outlive the idea.

Entrepreneurship is the practice of spotting an unmet need and building something — a product, service or organisation — to meet it, while carrying the risk of failure. Talks in this track cover starting up from a small city, bootstrapping without funding, freelancing as a first business, and building teams that can scale.

v

HUMAN PSYCHOLOGY

Why we think, feel and behave the way we do.

Human psychology is the study of the mind and behaviour — motivation, habit, emotion, memory and decision-making. This track looks at student mental health, burnout and resilience, the psychology of procrastination, and how understanding your own mind makes you better at everything else.

v

WOMEN LEADERSHIP

Women shaping decisions, institutions and culture.

Women leadership is about the visibility, access and authority of women in places where decisions get made — classrooms, boardrooms, sports fields and public life. Speakers share what it takes to lead in spaces that were not designed for them, and why mixed leadership produces better outcomes for everyone.

v

SOCIAL IMPACT

Work measured by the change it leaves behind.

Social impact is the measurable effect an initiative has on a community — in education, health, climate, inclusion or opportunity. This track features people running grassroots projects, volunteer networks and nonprofits, and explains how to design work whose success is judged by lives changed, not revenue.

v

AI / TECH

The tools rewriting how a generation works.

Artificial intelligence and modern technology are reshaping study, work and creativity — from machine learning and data science to automation and the global remote economy. Talks here cover what AI can and cannot do, the skills that stay valuable, and how students in Pakistan can build for a global market from campus.
