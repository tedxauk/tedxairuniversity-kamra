WHAT IS TED?

TED is an annual event where some of the world's leading thinkers and doers are invited to share what they are most passionate about. "TED" stands for Technology, Entertainment, Design — three broad subject areas that are, collectively, shaping our future. The diverse audience of CEOs, scientists, creatives and philanthropists is almost as extraordinary as the speakers, who have included Bill Clinton, Bill Gates, Jane Goodall, Frank Gehry, Paul Simon, Sir Richard Branson, Philippe Starck and Bono.

TEDX AIR UNIVERSITY KAMRA

First Spark of Innovation in Attock. A two-day independently organised TEDx event at the Air University Aerospace & Aviation Campus, Kamra, on 23 – 24 September 2026 — bringing talks, panels and a musical night to students across the region.


Our mission is to cultivate a community of curious minds driven to explore groundbreaking ideas and challenge the ordinary. We believe the local community holds immense untapped potential. Through this platform, we are dedicated to spotlighting homegrown innovators while actively connecting our youth with diverse global perspectives. By merging technology, social impact, and culture, we provide an environment where meaningful conversations naturally transform into actionable change.
