MEET THE ORGANISERS

TEDxAirUniversityKamra is brought to life by a dedicated team of students, creators, and visionaries. Here are the minds working behind the curtain to spark innovation in Attock.

ORGANISERS

AALAYAN RAZA

ORGANISER

Entrepreneur • BSCS Student

Aalayan is the visionary Organiser behind TEDxAirUniversityKamra. A dynamic young entrepreneur, he is the founder of Coco Cafe and Happy Coco Gift Center in Attock. His dedication to his craft led him to Bangladesh, where he completed specialized cafe management training. Currently pursuing his BSCS at Air University Kamra, Aalayan blends his strong business acumen with technical expertise to bring this global platform to his community.

MUHAMMAD HASSAAN

CO-ORGANISER

News Anchor • Media Professional

Muhammad Hassaan is a 17-year-old award-winning youth leader and social impact changemaker. He is the Co-Organizer of TEDxAir University Kamra. As the Founder of Visionary Tech Society (VTS), he has educated thousands of students in AI and digital skills while advancing UN SDG 4: Quality Education. He also led the Indus Internship Programme (IIP), a national-level paid internship initiative that attracted students from 80+ educational institutions across 30+ cities of Pakistan. Hassaan serves as Vice President of the Attock Champions League (ACL), one of the district's largest youth-led sports events. He has also served as a judge for the UNESCO Global Youth Hackathon. He is an accomplished news anchor and journalist and holds a world record for being one of the youngest news anchors in the country.
